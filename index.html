WHAT WE TAKE IN TRADE

We buy and sell the following items. We give cash and/or store credit and guarantee to pay the most for your items. Our entire inventory is completely comprised of trade-ins, so if you haven’t traded with us before, we’re sure you’ll be pleased with our trade-in prices. Stock changes daily, because folks bring in fresh finds every day.

  • Classic video games — Atari to PS5, Xbox Series & Switch 2
  • Comic books
  • Funko Pops
  • PC games
  • Sports cards (game-used, autographed, rookie, inserts, boxes & packs)
  • Sports & wrestling memorabilia & novelty items
  • Wrestling figures & collectibles
  • Modern video games (Xbox One, PS4, Wii U)
  • DVDs & Blu-rays
  • iPhones, iPods, iPads, Android phones & tablets
  • Vintage action figures — loose & boxed
  • Vintage collectibles
  • Strategy guides
  • Video game manuals
  • Accessories for all systems (power cords, AV cords, controllers)
  • Anything else — just call and ask!

TRADE-IN PROGRAM

Have some unwanted wrestling or celebrity collectibles? Trade them in for store credit! Here’s how it works:

STEP 01

SEND YOUR LIST

Tell us what you have to trade in (we may ask for pictures) and we’ll send you an offer for store credit.

CALL OR TEXT ► 860-479-9223 · MESSAGE US
STEP 02

SHIP & GET CREDIT

Ship us your items and we’ll issue you store credit — you’ll receive your own personal code to use on our site.

Use your store credit on our site or Amazon store for any item — t-shirts, autographs, photos and more.

▶ WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR

  • Signed wrestling photos
  • Signed action figures, magazines & books (wrestling or celebrity)
  • Unsigned WWE or WCW promo photos
  • Wrestling DVDs or VHS
  • Video games (must include box and/or jewel case, and manual)

✖ WHAT WE’RE NOT LOOKING FOR

  • Any fake autographs — we flat out refuse fakes
  • Personalized items (including ones with personalizations removed)
  • Clothing
  • Unsigned, non-licensed photos
