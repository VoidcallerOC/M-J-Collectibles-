CONSOLE & COMPUTER REPAIR

We've been repairing consoles and disks since 2014. Our technicians are experts at what they do. Bring it by the shop for a quote.

CONSOLES

Red light, yellow light, no light. PS3 blinking-light repair, Blu-ray laser replacement, HDMI, disc drives and more — tested on the bench, not guessed at.
